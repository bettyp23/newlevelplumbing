Signs you may need water heater installation

Inconsistent hot water, water that does not reach the expected temperature, unusual changes in performance, or visible leakage around a water heater may indicate that it needs attention. These signs can have more than one cause. If you see water near the unit, avoid contact with electrical equipment and contact a plumber to assess the situation.
